Choosing the Right Stone for Your Kitchen Countertops 如何为厨房台面选择合适的石材

October 2026 · B&B Marble

Your countertop is the hardest-working surface in your kitchen and often the first thing people notice. The best stone for you depends on how you cook, how much upkeep you'll accept, and the look you want. There's no single right answer. 台面是厨房里使用最频繁的表面，往往也是人们第一眼注意到的地方。最适合您的石材，取决于您的烹饪习惯、愿意投入多少日常保养，以及您想要的整体风格。并没有唯一的标准答案。

Below, we compare the six materials Bay Area homeowners ask us about most: quartz, marble, quartzite, soapstone, porcelain, and sintered stone (such as Dekton). We start with a side-by-side table, then explain what each one is like to live with. 下面，我们将对比湾区业主最常咨询的六种材料：石英石、大理石、石英岩、皂石、瓷砖板材，以及烧结石（如 Dekton）。我们先用一张对比表格一览全貌，再逐一介绍每种材料在日常生活中的表现。

Price levels are relative and cover material, fabrication, and installation. Within any material, the specific slab, edge profile, cutouts, and kitchen layout often move the price more than the material category does. 价格等级为相对比较，涵盖材料、加工与安装费用。在同一种材料中，具体板材、边缘造型、开孔数量和厨房布局对价格的影响，往往比材料类别本身更大。

01 — Quartz01 — 石英石

Quartz 石英石

Quartz countertops are engineered stone: about 90% ground natural quartz bound with resin and pigments. Because they are manufactured, the color and pattern are consistent from slab to slab. Styles range from solid whites to designs that closely imitate Calacatta marble. 石英石台面属于人造石，由约 90% 的天然石英碎料与树脂和颜料结合制成。由于是工业化生产，每块板材的颜色与花纹都保持一致。款式从纯白素色到高度仿真的卡拉卡塔（Calacatta）大理石纹理，应有尽有。

Why homeowners love it: Quartz is non-porous, so it never needs sealing and resists stains from coffee, wine, and oil. It is also very hard and scratch resistant, which makes it one of the lowest-maintenance options for a busy family kitchen. 业主为何喜爱：石英石无孔隙，因此无需封釉处理，也不易被咖啡、红酒和油渍沾染。它硬度高、耐刮擦，是繁忙家庭厨房中保养最省心的选择之一。

Keep in mind: The resin can scorch or discolor under high heat, so always use a trivet for hot pans. Quartz can also yellow in direct sunlight, which makes it a poor choice for outdoor kitchens. 注意事项：其中的树脂在高温下可能烧焦或变色，放置热锅时请务必使用隔热垫。石英石在阳光直射下也可能泛黄，因此不适合户外厨房。

Best for: Busy households that want a clean, consistent look with almost no upkeep. 最适合：希望外观简洁统一、几乎无需保养的忙碌家庭。

02 — Marble02 — 大理石

Marble 大理石

Marble is a natural metamorphic stone, mostly calcite, and has been prized for centuries. Each slab is one of a kind, with soft, luminous veining that no manufactured surface quite matches. Popular choices include Carrara, Calacatta, and Statuario. 大理石是一种天然变质岩，主要成分为方解石，数百年来一直备受推崇。每一块板材都独一无二，柔和而通透的纹理是任何人造材料都难以比拟的。热门品种包括卡拉拉（Carrara）、卡拉卡塔（Calacatta）和雕刻白（Statuario）。

Why homeowners love it: Nothing else has marble's depth and character. It stays naturally cool, which bakers love for rolling dough, and it handles warm cookware well. 业主为何喜爱：大理石的层次感与气质无可替代。它天然保持凉爽，深受烘焙爱好者青睐，非常适合擀面；对温热的炊具也有良好的耐受性。

Keep in mind: Marble is relatively soft and reacts with acids. Lemon juice, wine, tomato sauce, and vinegar can leave dull marks called etching. Sealing helps prevent stains, but it does not stop etching. A honed (matte) finish hides etching far better than a polished one. Over time marble develops a patina, which many owners consider part of its charm. 注意事项：大理石质地相对较软，且会与酸性物质发生反应。柠檬汁、红酒、番茄酱和醋都可能留下失去光泽的痕迹，即所谓的"酸蚀"。封釉处理有助于防污，但无法防止酸蚀。亚光（哑面）处理比抛光面更能掩盖酸蚀痕迹。随着时间推移，大理石会形成一层自然的包浆，许多业主认为这正是它的魅力所在。

Best for: Homeowners who want timeless natural beauty and are comfortable with a surface that shows its life. 最适合：追求永恒自然之美、并乐于接受台面留下生活痕迹的业主。

03 — Quartzite03 — 石英岩

Quartzite 石英岩

Quartzite is a natural stone formed when sandstone is transformed by heat and pressure. Don't confuse it with quartz: quartzite comes straight from the quarry, with no resin. Many quartzites, such as Taj Mahal and Mont Blanc, have the soft veining of marble with much greater durability. 石英岩是砂岩在高温高压作用下变质形成的天然石材。请勿将其与石英石混淆：石英岩直接开采自矿山，不含任何树脂。许多石英岩品种，如泰姬玛哈（Taj Mahal）和白朗峰（Mont Blanc），拥有大理石般柔和的纹理，耐用性却高出许多。

Why homeowners love it: True quartzite is about as hard as glass, so it resists scratches, etching, and heat far better than marble. It gives you the look of natural stone without as much worry. 业主为何喜爱：真正的石英岩硬度与玻璃相当，在耐刮擦、抗酸蚀和耐热方面都远胜大理石。它让您拥有天然石材的美感，却少了许多顾虑。

Keep in mind: Quartzite is porous, so it should be sealed periodically. Its hardness also makes it slow and demanding to cut, which raises fabrication cost. And some stones sold as "quartzite" are actually softer dolomite or marble. Before you commit, ask for a sample, or ask your fabricator to test that it resists scratching and etching. 注意事项：石英岩有孔隙，需要定期封釉。它的高硬度也使切割费时费力，从而提高加工成本。此外，部分以"石英岩"名义出售的石材实际上是较软的白云石或大理石。在做决定之前，请索取样品，或请您的加工商测试其是否真正耐刮擦、抗酸蚀。

Best for: Homeowners who want the look of marble with natural-stone durability, and have the budget for it. 最适合：想要大理石外观、又看重天然石材耐用性，并且预算充足的业主。

04 — Soapstone04 — 皂石

Soapstone 皂石

Soapstone is a natural stone rich in talc, which gives it a smooth, velvety feel. Colors run from soft gray to deep charcoal, often with subtle green undertones and light veining. It has long been used in farmhouse kitchens and science labs. 皂石是一种富含滑石的天然石材，触感顺滑细腻、如丝绒一般。颜色从柔和的浅灰到深炭灰不等，常带有淡淡的绿色底调和轻盈的纹理。长期以来，它一直被用于农舍风格厨房和科学实验室。

Why homeowners love it: Soapstone is dense and chemically inert. Acids won't etch it, and it doesn't need sealing. It also handles hot pans with ease. Scratches can be sanded out or blended in with a little mineral oil. 业主为何喜爱：皂石结构致密、化学性质稳定，不会被酸性物质腐蚀，也无需封釉。它能轻松应对热锅。表面划痕可以打磨去除，或用少许矿物油淡化。

Keep in mind: Soapstone is soft, so it scratches and dents more easily than other stones. It also darkens over time; regular mineral oil gives an even, deeper color. Slabs tend to be smaller, so larger kitchens may need more seams, and the color range is limited. 注意事项：皂石质地较软，比其他石材更容易出现划痕和凹痕。它的颜色也会随时间逐渐变深；定期涂抹矿物油可让颜色均匀加深。皂石板材尺寸通常较小，较大的厨房可能需要更多拼缝，而且可选颜色有限。

Best for: Homeowners who want a warm, lived-in look and a low-fuss surface that ages gracefully. 最适合：喜欢温暖、有生活气息的风格，并希望台面省心耐用、随岁月优雅老化的业主。

05 — Porcelain05 — 瓷砖板材

Porcelain 瓷砖板材

Porcelain slabs are made from refined clay and minerals fired at extremely high temperatures. The result is a thin, dense surface, often 12 mm thick, available in large slabs. Advanced printing lets porcelain closely mimic marble, concrete, and other natural stones. 瓷砖板材由精制黏土和矿物质经超高温烧制而成，形成轻薄而致密的表面，厚度通常为 12 毫米，并提供大尺寸板材。借助先进的印刷技术，瓷砖板材可以高度还原大理石、混凝土及其他天然石材的质感。

Why homeowners love it: Porcelain is non-porous, so it resists stains and never needs sealing. It handles heat well and won't fade in sunlight, so it works indoors and out. Its thin profile also suits sleek, modern designs, full-height backsplashes, and waterfall islands. 业主为何喜爱：瓷砖板材无孔隙，防污且无需封釉。它耐热性好，在阳光下也不会褪色，室内室外皆宜。纤薄的外形尤其适合简约现代的设计、整墙背板和瀑布岛台。

Keep in mind: Porcelain is brittle, and its edges can chip if struck by a heavy pot. On many slabs the pattern is printed on the surface, so edges are usually mitered to carry the look around the corner. Cutting it cleanly takes specialized equipment and an experienced team, so choose a fabricator with a strong porcelain portfolio. 注意事项：瓷砖板材质地较脆，边缘若被重锅撞击可能崩边。许多板材的花纹仅印在表面，因此边缘通常采用斜角拼接，让纹理自然延续到转角。要切割得干净利落，需要专业设备和经验丰富的团队，请选择拥有丰富瓷砖板材案例的加工商。

Best for: Modern kitchens, outdoor kitchens, and homeowners who want a low-maintenance stone look in a slim profile. 最适合：现代风格厨房、户外厨房，以及希望以纤薄外形呈现石材质感、且易于保养的业主。

06 — Sintered Stone06 — 烧结石

Sintered stone (Dekton) 烧结石（Dekton）

Sintered stone, also called ultra-compact surface, is made by fusing natural minerals under intense heat and pressure, speeding up a process that takes nature thousands of years. Dekton by Cosentino is the best-known brand; Neolith is another. Slabs come in several thicknesses, from thin panels to 20 mm and 30 mm countertops. 烧结石又称超致密表面材料，是将天然矿物在高温高压下熔合而成，相当于把大自然需要数千年才能完成的过程大大加速。Cosentino 旗下的 Dekton 是最知名的品牌，Neolith 也是常见品牌之一。板材有多种厚度可选，从轻薄面板到 20 毫米和 30 毫米的台面板。

Why homeowners love it: Sintered stone is one of the toughest surfaces available. It resists scratches, stains, heat, and UV light, needs no sealing, and performs equally well in a busy kitchen or an outdoor one. The color range is broad, from bold stone looks to soft concretes and solid colors. 业主为何喜爱：烧结石是目前最坚固的台面材料之一。它耐刮擦、防污、耐高温、抗紫外线，无需封釉，无论在繁忙的室内厨房还是户外厨房都同样出色。颜色选择丰富，从大胆的石材纹理到柔和的混凝土质感和纯色，一应俱全。

Keep in mind: Like porcelain, it can chip at the edges under a hard impact. Its hardness makes it among the most demanding materials to cut, requiring specialized tooling and an experienced fabricator. It also sits at the higher end of the price range. 注意事项：与瓷砖板材一样，烧结石在受到猛烈撞击时边缘可能崩裂。它的高硬度使其成为最难切割的材料之一，需要专业刀具和经验丰富的加工商。其价格也处于较高区间。

Best for: Homeowners who want maximum durability and a modern look, indoors or out. 最适合：追求极致耐用与现代外观的业主，室内室外皆宜。

How to choose 如何选择

Start with how you actually use your kitchen, then narrow by look and budget: 先从您实际如何使用厨房出发，再根据外观和预算逐步缩小范围：

  • You cook often and want minimal upkeep: quartz, porcelain, or sintered stone.经常下厨、希望尽量少保养：石英石、瓷砖板材或烧结石。
  • You love the look of marble but worry about wear: quartzite, or a marble-look porcelain or quartz.喜欢大理石的外观，却担心磨损：石英岩，或仿大理石纹理的瓷砖板材或石英石。
  • You want the real thing and welcome a patina: marble, ideally with a honed finish.想要真正的天然大理石，并欣赏岁月包浆：大理石，最好选择亚光面处理。
  • You put hot pans down without thinking: soapstone, quartzite, porcelain, or sintered stone.习惯随手放下热锅：皂石、石英岩、瓷砖板材或烧结石。
  • You're planning an outdoor kitchen: porcelain or sintered stone.正在规划户外厨房：瓷砖板材或烧结石。

Whatever you choose, the quality of fabrication and installation matters as much as the stone itself. Harder and thinner materials, especially quartzite, porcelain, and sintered stone, demand specialized equipment and experience to cut cleanly and seam well. 无论您选择哪种石材，加工与安装的品质都与石材本身同样重要。越坚硬、越轻薄的材料，尤其是石英岩、瓷砖板材和烧结石，越需要专业设备与丰富经验，才能切割整齐、拼缝精准。
